ABOUT

EA Control Tech designs practical industrial control and embedded hardware for automation, prototyping, and system integration applications — from individual I/O boards to complete modular PLC systems.

Our products are engineered to integrate reliably into demanding environments — from factory floors to OEM equipment — with a focus on well-documented, modular designs that engineers can trust and customize.

Every board and system ships with thorough documentation, connector pinouts, and wiring guidelines, making integration straightforward for both embedded and industrial engineers.

  • STM32-based embedded controllers
  • 24VDC industrial power inputs
  • RS485, CAN, SPI, I²C communication
  • Digital I/O expansion via MCP23017 / MCP23008
  • Analog I/O with 4–20mA, 0–10V & RTD support
  • Full schematics & manuals provided
  • Available on Tindie
